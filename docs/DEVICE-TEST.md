# Device tests

The simulator decodes no video and AVPlayer shows no picture there, so playback is proven only on a real iPhone: iOS 27, unplugged, Low Power Mode off, against the home Plex server. The IDs in brackets point at the video engine's device test sheets (videoengine `docs/ENGINE.md`, "Device test sheets"), which list the exact clips.

Each check records three things:

- **Diagnostics:** in the player, More › Playback Info › Copy Diagnostics, pasted into the report. It carries the route and why, the stream, the engine's recent measurements (`perf-*`, `remux-*`, `hw-*` lines) and the phone's decoders.
- **Stats:** More › Stats for Nerds (or a long press on the title) shows the same lines live; its Copy button copies them at that moment.
- **Screenshots** where a check is about what the screen shows. HDR brightness does not survive a screenshot; describe it instead.

## Checks

1. **Direct play.** A movie in MP4 or MOV, H.264 or HEVC with AAC. Pass: stats Route "Direct Play" with no Why line; it plays and seeks to 10, 50 and 90 % without a stall. Note the seconds to the first picture. Paste Diagnostics.
2. **MKV remux, HEVC.** A 1080p HEVC MKV and a 4K HDR10 MKV, five cold starts each, then five far seeks and three near seeks, then ten minutes of the 4K one [P1–P3]. Pass: Route "Repackaged on the phone", Why "MKV file", Picture ends "hardware decode"; Remux lead stays above 0 s; Heat at most Fair; Frames shows no stalls. Paste Diagnostics after the ten minutes. On a Mac, Console with subsystem `VideoEngine` shows the start signposts [P4].
3. **DTS and TrueHD.** One MKV with DTS, one with TrueHD, ideally the one-tone-per-channel clips [T4]; then AC-3, E-AC-3 (Atmos on AirPods Pro), FLAC and AAC files [T1–T3, T5–T7]. Pass: DTS and TrueHD show Audio "… converted" and every tone comes from its own speaker; AC-3 and E-AC-3 show "copied"; lip sync holds. Paste Diagnostics for DTS and for TrueHD.
4. **HDR and Dolby Vision.** HDR10, HLG and HDR10+ files [F1–F4, F12], then Dolby Vision profiles 5, 7 and 8.1 [F5–F11]. Pass: Picture names the format; highlights brighten past SDR white; profile 7 plays as HDR10 (Why "Dolby Vision profile 7 plays as HDR10"); no black picture or green tint; no error. Paste Diagnostics for each Dolby Vision profile.
5. **Anime with a dub and subtitles.** An episode with Japanese and English audio, full English subtitles and an English dub transcript, and an intro and credits marker. Pass: the Tracks menu picks the audio from Settings › Audio & Subtitles; switching to the dub with Follow the Audio on picks the dub transcript; Skip Intro counts down 5 s then skips, and Watch Intro stops it; Up Next counts down 5 s at the credits, and Watch Credits stops it. Subtitles: cues on time after seeks, styled ASS keeps italics [S1–S3, S5, S8]. Screenshot the Skip Intro pill and the Up Next card.
6. **Picture subtitles.** A Blu-ray MKV with PGS (and a forced-only PGS track), and a DVD MKV with VobSub [S6, S7]. Pass: subtitles sit in the picture, not on the black bars, in both orientations; forced-only shows only the forced lines; pause, seek and 2× keep them right. Screenshot one PGS line.
7. **Picture in Picture.** Start Picture in Picture from More during an MKV, then go to the Home Screen [S4]. Pass: video keeps playing in the window; text subtitles show in it; picture subtitles are absent (expected); returning restores the full player at the same time.
8. **Lock Screen.** Play an episode, then lock the iPhone. Pass: sound continues; the Lock Screen shows the poster, title and "S2 · E5 · Name"; play, pause, skip back and skip forward work and the time stays right after each.
9. **AirPlay.** AirPlay to an Apple TV, once from a direct-play file and once from an MKV [S9]. Pass: a tap anywhere on the AirPlay circle opens the system picker; the glyph turns accent once the TV plays; the direct-play file plays on the TV as it is; the MKV moves to the server within a few seconds at the same time (Route "Repackaged by the server" or "Converted by the server", Why "AirPlay needs a server stream"), because the TV cannot reach the stream the phone makes. Paste Diagnostics from the MKV case.
10. **Stats overlay.** Open it during a remux and during a server conversion (an item over the quality limit, or AirPlay above). Pass: the numbers move twice a second; Transcode shows the server's speed and decisions; taps and double-taps on the picture still work through the panel; Copy gives a light tap and VoiceOver says "Copied"; two columns in landscape, one in portrait; Frames shows no new drops while the panel is open. Screenshot both orientations.
11. **Hardware.** Once per iPhone model: paste the Hardware lines of Diagnostics [H1]; play the HDR files above and the VP9, MPEG-2, VC-1, Hi10P H.264 and AV1 clips, noting which route each takes [H2, H3]; then ten minutes of 4K with Low Power Mode on [H4]. Pass: each clip the Hardware lines call hardware-decodable plays on the phone, and the rest go to the server; with Low Power Mode on, the Heat line says so and playback carries on.
12. **Chromecast.** Turn on Settings › Chromecast, then cast from the player's route popover to a Chromecast (and a Chromecast Ultra or Google TV Streamer if there is one): once a direct-play MP4, once an MKV, once with subtitles on. Pass: the receiver is listed within a few seconds; the MP4 plays as it is and the MKV and the subtitled one stream from the server with the subtitle burned in; play, pause, seek, skip and the Lock Screen control the TV; Skip Intro and Up Next still work; the item is marked watched near the end; This iPhone brings it back paused at the same time; closing the player stops the TV. Untested on hardware so far.
